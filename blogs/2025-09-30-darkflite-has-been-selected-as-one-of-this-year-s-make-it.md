We’re proud to share that darkflite has been selected as one of this year’s “Make It in Michigan” awardees. The grant will support the development of advanced UAS capabilities for government end-users. Through the “Make It in Michigan” Mobility Prototyping Grant Program, more than $2 million has been awarded to accelerate the next generation of mobility and defense innovations.
