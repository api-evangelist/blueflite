The Northern Michigan drone delivery pilot report is now available for download. The report summarizes the outcomes, insights, and key learnings from the pilot program conducted in collaboration with regional healthcare, economic development, and aviation partners. It highlights operational performance, regulatory considerations, and the potential role of drone logistics in supporting healthcare delivery and advanced air mobility in Northern Michigan.…
