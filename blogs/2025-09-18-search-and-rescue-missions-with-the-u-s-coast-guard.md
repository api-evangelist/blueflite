On 10 September 2025, blueflite flew alongside the U.S. Coast Guard at Yuba Airport in Grand Traverse County, building on our joint demonstration in May at Discovery Pier in Traverse City. These exercises highlight how drones can strengthen public safety and enhance search and rescue capabilities nationwide.
