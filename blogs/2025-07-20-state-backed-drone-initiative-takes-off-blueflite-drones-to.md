DETROIT, MI – July 17, 2025 – blueflite, Jack Demmer Ford, Centrepolis Accelerator, Airspace Link, and DroneUp have joined forces to launch a pioneering drone delivery initiative focused on transporting automotive parts by air. Backed by a $740,000 grant from Michigan’s Advanced Air Mobility (AAM) Activation Fund, the project will pilot the use of autonomous drones to deliver high-demand car parts…
